01 / THE VISIONGAMEPLAY FIRST

Football with
a pulse.

Qolo is an ambitious, fully offline 3D football game for Android. The idea is simple: make every turn, touch and strike feel earned, then follow one player's path through a living fictional football world.

We are not claiming a finished game. Right now we are laying the groundwork for a compact match loop, clear systems and performance tests on real devices.

Momentum-led movement and ball response come before career breadth.
